# TradePilot AI — Project Context

## 1. Project Goal

TradePilot AI is an AI-powered cross-border market research assistant.

It helps small cross-border sellers and international trade professionals turn fragmented market information into structured, evidence-backed market insights.

This project is primarily an AI portfolio project designed to demonstrate:

- product thinking
- business analysis
- AI workflow design
- data-informed decision making
- AI coding ability

Employment value is more important than technical complexity.

## 2. Target Users

Primary users:

- small cross-border sellers
- international trade professionals
- TikTok Shop operators
- Shopee operators
- Lazada operators
- independent e-commerce sellers

## 3. Core Problem

Cross-border market research is fragmented.

Users often need to manually collect:

- competitor information
- pricing
- customer reviews
- product positioning
- market signals

and then convert them into business decisions.

This process is slow, repetitive, and difficult to structure.

## 6. Market Report Structure

The V0.1 report contains:

1. Market Snapshot
2. Customer Needs
3. Customer Pain Points
4. Competitor Landscape
5. Market Opportunities
6. Risks & Limitations
7. Recommended Actions

## 7. Core Product Differentiation

The core differentiation is the Evidence Mechanism.

Important AI insights should be traceable to supporting evidence.

The product should visibly distinguish:

DATA FACT

from

AI INFERENCE

Example:

Data Fact:
Median competitor price = RM79.

AI Inference:
RM79 may represent a viable mid-market entry price.

## 10. Design Direction

TradePilot should feel like a modern B2B SaaS analytics product.

Design characteristics:

- clean
- professional
- international
- data-focused
- minimal
- strong visual hierarchy

Avoid:

- playful consumer-app styling
- excessive gradients
- unnecessary animation
- generic chatbot interfaces
- visual clutter

## 11. Success Criteria

TradePilot V0.1 is successful when:

- users can complete the full demo flow
- the market report is easy to understand
- evidence-backed AI logic is clearly visible
- the demo can be deployed publicly
- recruiters can understand the product in under five minutes
