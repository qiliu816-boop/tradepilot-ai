# TradePilot AI

**AI-powered cross-border market research assistant for evidence-backed market opportunity analysis.**

TradePilot helps small cross-border sellers and international trade professionals turn fragmented competitor data, pricing signals and customer feedback into structured market insights and actionable business recommendations.

## Live Demo

https://tradepilot-ai-livid.vercel.app

---

## Why TradePilot?

Cross-border market research is often fragmented.

Before entering a new market, sellers may need to manually review:

- competitor listings
- product prices
- customer reviews
- product positioning
- market signals
- spreadsheets and notes

The problem is not only collecting information.

The harder part is turning scattered evidence into a clear business decision.

TradePilot was designed to make this process more structured, traceable and decision-oriented.

---

## Core Product Idea

TradePilot transforms market evidence into a structured decision workflow:

**Market Evidence → Customer Signals → Competitor Analysis → AI Interpretation → Business Action**

Instead of presenting a generic AI-generated answer, TradePilot separates observable market evidence from AI interpretation.

This makes the reasoning behind key recommendations easier to understand.

---

## Evidence Mechanism

The core product differentiation is the **Evidence Mechanism**.

Important conclusions are structured as:

**DATA FACT**

Observable information from the dataset.

↓

**EVIDENCE**

Supporting reviews, prices, themes or competitor information.

↓

**AI INFERENCE**

Interpretation generated from the available evidence.

↓

**BUSINESS IMPLICATION**

What the insight could mean for the business.

↓

**ACTION**

A concrete next step for market validation.

### Example

**Data Fact**

Median competitor price: RM79

**AI Inference**

RM79 may represent a viable mid-market entry position.

**Business Implication**

Compete through product differentiation rather than deep price discounting.

**Recommended Action**

Test the RM69–RM79 price range before scaling inventory.

## Product Flow

### 1. Define the Market Question

Users enter:

- product information
- target market
- selling platform
- product cost
- target selling price
- business goal

### 2. Structure Market Evidence

TradePilot organises:

- competitor data
- pricing information
- customer needs
- recurring pain points

### 3. Simulate AI Analysis

The V0.1 workflow visualises five analysis stages:

1. Reading Market Evidence
2. Extracting Customer Signals
3. Comparing Competitors
4. Evaluating Market Opportunity
5. Generating Recommendations

### 4. Generate the Market Report

The final report contains:

- Market Snapshot
- Customer Needs
- Customer Pain Points
- Competitor Landscape
- Market Opportunities
- Risks & Limitations
- Recommended Actions
- Decision Summary

## Key Features

### Market Opportunity Analysis

Summarises pricing signals, competition and potential market-entry opportunities.

Identifies recurring customer needs from review themes.

Highlights common complaints and potential product differentiation opportunities.

## Design Principles

TradePilot V0.1 follows several product principles:

- evidence before interpretation
- clear distinction between fact and inference
- visible risks and limitations
- simple decision-oriented workflow
- no unnecessary chatbot interface
- no unsupported AI claims
- actionable recommendations instead of long AI-generated text

## Development Workflow

The project uses a structured AI Coding workflow.

Repository context is maintained through:

- `AGENTS.md`
- `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`
- `CURRENT_TASK.md`
- `CHANGELOG.md`

Each Codex session reads the project context and current task before making changes.

This helps keep product scope, technical decisions and development history consistent across sessions.

## Limitations

TradePilot V0.1 is a portfolio MVP.

Current limitations include:

- mock rather than live marketplace data
- no real marketplace API integration
- no real file upload or CSV parsing
- no live LLM analysis
- no authentication
- no database
- no report history
- no PDF export

The current version focuses on validating the product flow, information architecture and evidence-backed AI concept.
